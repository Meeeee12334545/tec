01Why this work

The Gap Is Already Being Paid For.

The difference between a well resourced school and a stretched one is rarely commitment. It is time and money. Schools that can afford specialist capacity on staff buy it. Schools that cannot absorb the same work through teachers’ unpaid hours and stretched expertise.

We start from the position that your staff are already doing their best. The job is not to tell a school it is failing. It is to improve the tools and processes available, at a fraction of the time and cost it would take to build the same capability alone.

Costs a school already carries

  • Training a new teacher through university
  • Onboarding that teacher into your systems and culture
  • Turnover: recruitment, lost institutional knowledge, disruption to students
  • The downstream cost sitting in your wellbeing and attrition data

Advisory support is not an added expense. It is a faster, lower cost way to close a capability gap the school is already funding in other forms.

02The economics

The Saving Is Larger Than The Fee.

Every engagement is scoped so that the hours and costs it removes exceed what it costs to run, in the term it is delivered and in every year that follows. That test is applied before you commit, not after. Where the numbers do not clear it, we say so and reduce the scope or decline the work.

What the school pays today

Absorbed, Recurring, Uncosted

  • Teacher hours spent planning, moderating and reporting outside load
  • Leadership time spent building capability the school does not yet hold
  • Replacing a teacher who leaves: recruitment, induction, lost knowledge
  • A specialist salary and superannuation, where the role is filled internally
  • Work repeated each year because the process was never written down

What an engagement costs

Fixed, Bounded & Final

  • One fee, agreed in writing before any work begins
  • No salary, no superannuation, no recruitment, no ongoing commitment
  • Delivered inside a defined window that suits your calendar
  • Templates and processes the school keeps, reuses and owns
  • Capability that stays with your staff after we leave

The Return

In The First Term

Nobody starts a unit from a blank document. Nobody rewrites a rubric that already exists somewhere. Nobody loses an evening looking for the file that was on the shared drive last year. Those hours come back inside the same term, to every teacher the change touches.

Every Year After

The plans, templates and processes stay in the school. Term 1 next year opens on a finished document rather than an empty one, and when a teacher moves on, what they knew stays behind. The fee is paid once. The hours come back every year.

Weigh the fee against one year of the hours it gives back and it is the smaller figure. Weigh it against five and there is not much left to weigh.

We only propose work that pays for itself inside the first term and keeps paying every year after. If an engagement cannot clear that bar, we will say so before you commit.
